I have spent twenty-one years in the space where energy strategy meets industrial reality — where a decision made in a boardroom has to survive contact with a construction site, a contractor interface, and a team of engineers under pressure.

## The career arc

My career has moved from hands-on automation and instrumentation engineering through project execution, to engineering management of major capital investments in renewable fuels, oil & gas, and energy transition. I have worked from both sides of the table — as owner and as contractor — across FEL, FEED, EPC, EPCM, and LSTK environments. The projects have ranged from feasibility studies to €300M+ refinery investments across Finland, the Netherlands, Singapore, and the Middle East.

The pattern I have followed, even before I could articulate it, is to take on roles where the system is unclear and someone needs to build the structure that lets the work get done.

## What I have learned

Most delivery failures are not technical. They are structural — unclear ownership, misaligned engineering and business expectations, teams without the capability or confidence to make decisions early enough to matter.

My work has increasingly been about fixing those structural problems. Building governance where there was none. Developing engineers into genuine owners of their scope. Creating the conditions for organisations to deliver with less chaos and more lasting capability.

This shows up in concrete ways — the Equipment Package Unit Manager role I created to fix accountability gaps in long-lead procurement, the 5C Design Review Framework I developed for multidisciplinary engineering quality, the standardisation of engineering roles across three reporting levels at Neste. These are not personal contributions. They are organisational interventions — the kind a leader is paid to make.

## What drives me

Building the people and systems that outlast any single project. Not delivering one thing well, but leaving an organisation genuinely stronger than I found it — with better habits, clearer standards, and leaders who did not exist before.

## Where I am now

Currently leading engineering delivery for major renewable refinery investments at Neste — including Rotterdam projects where engineering decisions sit at the intersection of business continuity, environmental compliance, and refinery license-to-operate outcomes.

Alongside this, I am completing an Executive MBA at Quantic School of Business & Technology — deliberately building the commercial, financial, and strategic layer that complements two decades of engineering and project leadership.

## What comes next

I am focused on senior engineering and organisational leadership roles — programme management, portfolio governance, and capital investment leadership — where strategy needs to be translated into disciplined execution, and where the quality of the team and the system matters as much as the outcome of any individual project.
