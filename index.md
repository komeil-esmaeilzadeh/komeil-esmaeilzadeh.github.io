## Senior engineering leader at the intersection of energy investment, capital projects, and operational reliability.

I have spent twenty-one years building and leading engineering teams in oil & gas, renewable fuels, and energy infrastructure — from instrumentation engineering on national projects, through EPC delivery in the Gulf, to multidisciplinary investment leadership at Neste in Finland.

What I care about is not single projects. It is building the engineering capability, the structures, and the people that outlast any one delivery — so the organisation is genuinely stronger than I found it.

This site is where I share how I think about that work.
